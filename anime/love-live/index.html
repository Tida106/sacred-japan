Love Live! Pilgrimage: Nozomi's Shrine in Kanda

ラブライブ！ 聖地巡礼

Kanda Shrine, the training stairs and a few stops in Akihabara, five minutes apart

Many people come to Akihabara for electronics and anime shops and never realize there is a major shrine a few minutes' walk away. In Love Live! School Idol Project, Nozomi Tojo works part-time as a shrine maiden there, and the shrine has embraced it. If you are in Tokyo and want a pilgrimage that fits into a free afternoon, this is the easiest one I know.

💡 Quick Visitor Snapshot

  • The main stop: Kanda Shrine (Kanda Myojin), 2-16-2 Soto-Kanda, Chiyoda, Tokyo.
  • Getting there: about 5 minutes on foot from Ochanomizu, Shin-Ochanomizu or Suehirocho stations, or about 7 minutes from JR Akihabara Station (from a 2021 article; check current routes).
  • Official link: Love Live!-branded ema are sold, and the shrine named Nozomi an official mascot in 2015.
  • Time needed: 2 to 3 hours with the nearby stops.

Kanda Shrine: More Than a Backdrop

Kanda Shrine was built in 730 in a fishing village near modern Otemachi, moved in 1603 and again in 1616 to its current hill, and was rebuilt in concrete in 1934 after the 1923 Great Kanto Earthquake. It enshrines Onamuchi (Daikoku), Sukunabikona (Ebisu) and Taira no Masakado, so business people come to pray for prosperity.

Walk up from the street and the first thing you meet is the copper-green torii, with the red Zuishin-mon gate behind it. Look up when you pass under the gate, because the beams and pillars hold carved guardian beasts in gold and blue.

Before the main hall, stop at the purification basin. A bronze dragon pours the water, and the wooden ladles are for rinsing your hands. If you are unsure of the steps, my shrine etiquette guide walks through them.

It also sells talismans to protect electronic devices, a fitting detail next to Akihabara. Its great festival, the Kanda Matsuri, is held around May 15 in odd-numbered years and began in 1600, to celebrate Tokugawa Ieyasu's victory at Sekigahara.

Ema and Charms

Love Live!-branded ema are available for writing wishes. Write a real wish, and keep the fandom side to the design. For choosing and returning charms, see my omamori guide.

The shrine office (Saimu-sho) is where the ema, amulets and goshuin are handled. Come at dusk and the whole row glows, which is the best light for photos of the ema rack. Nearby, a heart-shaped stand is covered in tied fortune slips, a modern touch that suits the neighborhood.

The Stairs and the Walk

Myojin Otokozaka is the staircase near the shrine where the group is said to train. Go on a quiet morning and you will feel why it works as a training scene. After that, a short walk takes you to the Akihabara spots above. I would do the shrine first, while the grounds are calm.

The steps are steep and lined with lanterns, and they drop straight into the narrow streets of Soto-Kanda. Both photos below look down the same staircase, one in daylight at New Year, when the red banners read geishun (welcome, spring), and one in the blue hour, when the street glows orange at the bottom. If you climb up, count on a short, steady effort rather than a hike.

Come for the Festival

Kanda Matsuri is one of Tokyo's three great Shinto festivals, held in odd-numbered years, so the next one is in May 2027. A Daikoku festival is also held in January. Expect crowds.

During the festival, portable shrines (mikoshi) are carried through Akihabara's electric streets, which is a sight you will not forget. The first photo shows a procession in the neighborhood, and the second shows a decorated float arriving at the shrine in the miyairi, the entry of the festival's floats and mikoshi into the grounds. Book hotels early and plan to arrive well before the main events.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Love Live! shrine real?
Yes. Nozomi Tojo works as a shrine maiden at Kanda Shrine (Kanda Myojin) in the anime, and the shrine named her an official mascot in 2015.
How do I get to Kanda Shrine from Akihabara?
About 7 minutes on foot from JR Akihabara Station, or about 5 minutes from Ochanomizu, Shin-Ochanomizu or Suehirocho stations, according to a 2021 article.
Can I buy Love Live! ema there?
Love Live!-branded ema have been available for writing wishes. Check the shrine office for current designs.
When is the Kanda Festival?
Around May 15 in odd-numbered years, so the next is May 2027.
⛩️

Kanda Shrine has welcomed merchants for centuries, and now fans too. Visit for the girl in the shrine-maiden outfit, and stay for the old Edo story behind the hill.
