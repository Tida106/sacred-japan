Japanese Culture: Castles, Crafts, Anime & Regional Food

Explore the Japan that lives beyond its shrines and temples — fortresses built for warlords, crafts kept alive by master artisans, food shaped by mountain and sea, and the modern myths anime has built on top of all of it.

Sacred Japan is built around shrines and temples — but a full visit to Japan stretches far beyond them. The four cultural guides below explore the country's castles, its traditional crafts, the regional food that defines each part of the archipelago, and the anime pilgrimage spots that turn fans into travelers. Use them as starting points to plan a deeper trip.

A pilgrimage guide to 18 of Japan's most beloved anime locations — from Your Name's Tokyo staircase and Lake Suwa to Demon Slayer's Kamado Shrine in Fukuoka.
