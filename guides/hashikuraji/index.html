Hashikura-ji Temple: The Ascent of 700 Steps

Introduction

Clinging to the mountainside in Tokushima Prefecture, Hashikura-ji Temple is a sprawling, magnificent complex that feels suspended between earth and sky. Known as the inner sanctuary of the famous Konpira-san, it offers sweeping panoramic views of the Shikoku mountains. It is a destination that demands physical effort but rewards it with unparalleled spiritual grandeur.

The Trial

While a ropeway assists with the initial elevation, the true test remains: the "Hannya Shingyo" steps. To reach the main hall, you must climb over 700 grueling stone stairs. This exhausting physical ascent is a deliberate barrier, designed to test your devotion. Each step is a physical mantra, a process of sweating out worldly concerns and earning the right to stand in the sacred presence of the mountaintop.

The Reward

As you crest the final steps, gasping for air, the mountain opens up. You are surrounded by grand, ornate wooden halls framed by a spectacular display of autumn colors that stretch across the valley below. The physical exhaustion fades into a profound sense of achievement and awe. The vibrant leaves, viewed from this soaring elevation, look like a painting spread across the earth.

The Pilgrim's Guide

Wear sturdy, comfortable hiking shoes and carry plenty of water. The climb is genuinely taxing, so pace yourself and take breaks as needed. The ropeway operates on a specific schedule, so plan your descent carefully. When you reach the top, take a moment to catch your breath and fully absorb the magnificent scale of the architecture and the surrounding autumn landscape.
